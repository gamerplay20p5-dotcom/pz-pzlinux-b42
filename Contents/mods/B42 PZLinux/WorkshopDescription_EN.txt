Mod ID: B42_PZLinux42

Install matching versions on clients and the server, then restart. Back up the save first.
Local Market vehicle sales use the existing JeevesClaims integration. Without it, use item trading or disable vehicle sales in Sandbox. Multi Funcoes does not replace that integration automatically.
